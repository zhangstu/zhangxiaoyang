Career & education

Xiaoyang Zhang is a Research Scientist at the Alibaba-NTU Global e-Sustainability CorpLab, Nanyang Technological University (NTU). Before joining NTU, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where he received his Ph.D. in Computing in January 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Dan Wang. He holds a master’s degree in Communication and Computer Networks from the Polytechnic University of Turin and a bachelor’s degree in Optical Information Science and Technology from Xi’an Jiaotong University. Previously, he worked as a Data Analyst in the Knowledge Graph Group at ICBC Software Development Center and as an Algorithm Engineer in Huawei’s Consumer BG Communication Protocol Technology Department.
